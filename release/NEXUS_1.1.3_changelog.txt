Savior's Patch 1.1.3 - Nexus changelog (BBCode)

[size=4][b]1.1.3[/b][/size]

[b]Crash fix - NPC Spawning Distance: Extended.[/b] In very dense crowds (reported in Yusnaan's Reveler's Quarter at night, the moment the Flanitors turn hostile) the game could crash with Extended on. The game has a hard budget of 144 scene actors; Extended's larger crowd plus the entities a fight creates could use every one of them, and the next NPC to spawn crashed the game. The mod now watches that budget every frame and keeps a reserve free ([i]NpcActorReserve[/i] in SaviorsPatch.ini, default 32): when actors run short it thins the random crowd first, then falls back to the game's own spawn distances, and never goes below what the unmodded game shows. Once the fight is over the crowd fills back in within a second or two. Anything the game would show without the mod is still shown.

[b]Housekeeping.[/b] Existing SaviorsPatch.ini files carry over; the new key appears with its default on the next settings change. The crash log now records the actor budget at the time of a crash.
